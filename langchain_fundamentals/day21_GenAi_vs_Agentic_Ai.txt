LANGGRAPH - VIDEO 1: GENERATIVE AI VS AGENTIC AI

WHAT I LEARNED:

1. Generative AI:
   AI that generates new content - text, images, audio, video - mimicking 
   human-created output. Single input -> single output. Cannot take 
   autonomous action on its own.

2. Agentic AI:
   Goal-oriented AI - focused on completing a goal, not just generating 
   content. Can take actions, has memory, adapts, and can provide advice/
   decisions based on reasoning - not just a single response.

3. Key relationship (corrected):
   Agentic AI is NOT a subset of Generative AI, and Generative AI is not 
   a subset of Agentic AI either - rather, Agentic AI BUILDS ON TOP OF 
   Generative AI. An agent typically uses a generative model (the LLM) as 
   its reasoning engine, then adds planning, tool use, memory, and 
   autonomous action around it.

4. Practical connection to what I've built:
   Yesterday's Tool Calling + Agent code is a real example of Agentic AI - 
   the LLM (generative) decides what to do, but the tool execution, 
   looping, and goal completion is what makes it "agentic."
